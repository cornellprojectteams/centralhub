The tasks fall in one of three categories:

  • Open Work still waiting on your team.
  • Pending Work you have sent in. An admin still needs to look at your photo and confirm the job is done.
  • Recently completed Work an admin has already checked and closed.
  • Expand all Opens every card in the list you are looking at. Click on it. Click again to fold them.

Complete a task

To complete a task, click on it to expand it.

Click Add photos and pick a picture of how it looks now. You can also add an optional note for the admin.

Then click Complete.

What happens next

The photos you uploaded, and any note you added, get routed to the admin. Two things can happen from here.

They can send it back

The task will reappear on your Open tasks with a note explaining why.
