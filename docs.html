Running the admin side

The everyday jobs on the admin page, in the same order as the page. Anything that needs code, a script or a deployment is in the developer guide.

Start here Space Projects Equipment Teams Experiential Learning Lab Fabman Compliance Fleet Command Center

01Start here

  • Keep the admin link private. Anything opened from the admin page has admin controls and no passcode. Send students the staff page, never an admin link.
  • Find a tool. Press / and type. Enter opens the first result.
  • Big tiles open a panel. Full page opens the same view in a tab. Esc closes it.
  • Check the Command Center. A number on the gold button means something needs attention.
  • Practice first. The sandbox is a copy of everything on made-up data. Nothing you do there reaches a real team.

02Space

Staff report a problem in a team space. The team gets an email, reminders follow until it is fixed, the team sends a photo, and you approve it.

Deadlines

Respond within on the report form sets the deadline. Days count from the report.

Respond withinDeadlineReminders
Same daySame dayEvery day
2 to 3 business days3 daysOn the due date, then every 2 days
7 to 10 days10 daysOn the due date, then every 5 days
14 to 21 days21 daysOn the due date, then every 11 days
When time allowsNoneNone

Reminders pause while a photo waits for your review and stop once you approve it.

Daily jobs

Open Open issues dashboard. The chips switch between open, pending and completed.

  • Approve finished work. Pending jobs have the team's photos. Approve closes the job.
  • Send work back. Send back reopens the job with your reason. The team gets an email and reminders restart.
  • Close a job yourself. Mark complete closes an open job with no photo.
  • Fix a report. Edit changes the team, type or details. Moving it to another team emails that team.
  • Delete a report. Delete removes it for good. There is no undo.
  • Reopen an approved job. In the Space Status sheet, clear its Addressed at cell.
  • Send a team its list. Share a team's issue link gives a link to that team's open issues.

Who gets the email

The team's row on the Team Contacts tab: the team email and liaison, with the safety lead and Extra CC copied. Noah is copied on every email. Edit the row to change who gets it; it applies from the next email.

Reminder emails

Open Reminder emails to see what the system sent and what it sends next.

  • Next run. The reminders the next daily run sends. Open a row for the recipients, the reason and the email itself.
  • Later. Every other open job with its next reminder date. Paused means a photo is waiting for your review.
  • Sent. Every email, newest first, with the job's status now.
  • Flagged. Emails that failed to send, and any reminder dated after the team finished.

A team says it still gets reminders after finishing. Type the team in the filter and open its latest reminder under Sent. Now shows the job's status. An amber note gives the cause when there is one: the job was sent back, or the same problem was reported twice and one report is still open. Close a duplicate with Mark complete or Delete on the dashboard. The Email log tab of the Space Status sheet has every email since the log started. A reminder that is not there was not sent by this system.

Rules for the form and sheet

  • Adding a team: add it to the form's Responsible Team question and to Team Contacts with the exact same name. Then ask a developer to run fillTeamTokens.
  • Don't rename the form's questions or its Respond within choices. The script reads them by their wording.
  • Don't type in the columns the script fills: Notified at, Issue token, Addressed at, Last reminder, Completion photo, Completion note, Completed at, Sent back reason. The same goes for the Email log tab.

03Projects

Projects are bigger jobs, often for several people. Staff find them under Your work on the staff page.

  1. Create one in Assign & track projects: title, description, assignees, and a before photo if you like.
  2. Someone joins. It moves to In Progress.
  3. They finish it with an after photo and the hours. It moves to Pending.
  4. You approve it, or send it back with a reason.

Proposals

Staff post ideas from Propose an improvement and rate each other's. Review proposals opens the board with your controls.

  • Ratings. Everyone rates Impact and Effort from 1 to 5, once per proposal, never their own.
  • Automatic projects. Three counted ratings with an average Impact of 3.5 or more turns a proposal into a project.
  • Your controls. Approve now, Decline (keeps the record), Edit, or Delete (for junk).

04Equipment

The inventory lists every tool and supply in the shops: where it is, its condition, how many there are and its maintenance. Staff see the same board without the editing controls.

Adding and editing items

  • Add an item. Add item, then fill in the name, category, team, location and Quantity. Supplies also take a reorder level.
  • Add several in a row. Add and start another saves and opens a new form with the same category, location and team.
  • Add a photo. In the form, or from the item's page. On a phone you can take one on the spot.
  • Update a count. On a supply's page, Update the count.
  • Log maintenance. On the item's page: what was wrong, what was done, by whom, and the next check date.
  • Take an item out of service. Edit it and set Status to Maintenance, or Retired.
  • See what's new. The Recently added filter shows the last 30 days. Each item shows when it was added and by whom.

Letting teams add their own items

  1. At the bottom of the inventory, open Team links and click Show team links.
  2. Copy the team's link and send it to the team.
  3. Team members add their tools and supplies with quantities and photos. They can only add and edit their own team's items.

Reset all links stops every old link from working. Send teams their new links afterwards.

Labels and the sheet

  • Print labels. Print labels makes a QR label for each item. Item IDs never change, so labels stay good.
  • The spreadsheet. One row per item on Inventory Master. Don't type in Status, Next Maintenance, Item Link or Date Added; they fill themselves in.
  • Test data. Load test data adds sample items you can practice on. Remove test data only removes those.

05Teams

Team member photos shows the headshots from every team's website, with filters for team and lead, member or advisor.

  • Find people. Pick a team, a role, or search by name. Download CSV saves what is shown.
  • Get new photos. Press Update from team sites. It takes 5 to 10 minutes and says what changed. You can leave the page while it runs. It also runs by itself on the 1st of each month.
  • Graduates. Nothing to do. People are hidden from June 1 of their class year. Anyone no longer on their team's site is hidden as former. Turn on Graduated & former to see them.
  • Fix a person or add one by hand. Open Photo database on the admin page and edit it on GitHub. The steps are in the developer guide.

The photos are only as current as each team's website. If a team hasn't updated its site, ask the team to.

06Experiential Learning Lab

ELL staff fill in the shift summary form at the end of each shift. View shift responses opens the answers. The Command Center flags safety problems and supply shortages from the latest shift.

07Fabman

Open Fabman goes to the Fabman admin site. Bulk changes happen in the Fabman members sheet.

  • New sign-ups. Agreements signed in the Canvas course become Fabman accounts within a minute, on the FabMan Agreement tab. If a row's Fabman status starts with Error or Has an account, fix the row (usually a mistyped NetID) and clear the status. Will retry fixes itself.
  • Catch up after an outage. Fabman sign-ups menu, Process sign-ups since a date.
  • Change many accounts at once. On the Actions tab, put emails in column A and press a button: machines, mills, apron color, supervisor, lock, activate, delete or invite.
  • Remove graduates. Flag graduates for review lists them, each set to Lock. Untick anyone to keep, then Process graduates.
  • Don't rename the FabMan Agreement tab or its headers. The Canvas survey writes to them by name.

08Compliance

  • Building access & training records. Usage agreements and waivers by location, plus Workday safety training.
  • Project Teams workspaces. Every team space with its room, waivers, safety plan and yearly renewal.
  • Summer contact list. Who to reach on each team over the summer.

09Fleet

Student drivers & reservations opens the sheet for the Tacoma (MBN-4258): driver approvals on Approval, bookings on Log.

Removing graduated drivers

  1. In the sheet, Driver cleanup > 1. List graduated drivers.
  2. On the Drivers to remove tab, delete the row of anyone to keep.
  3. 2. Delete listed drivers to remove their rows, or 2. Remove listed drivers (access) to remove their access.

10Command Center

One screen with what needs attention in every area: overdue space issues, ELL flags, Fabman members waiting or graduated, missing access records, Tacoma double bookings and driver applications, and mill room orders and locks. The number on the gold button counts them. It refreshes every few minutes.
