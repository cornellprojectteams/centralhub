Admin guide

Running the admin side

What each part of the admin page is for and how to do the everyday jobs in it. Changing how something works (code, scripts, deploying) is covered in the developer guide.

01The admin page

The admin page holds the dashboards, forms and sheets for each area. It is not linked from the staff page. The dashboards it opens come up with admin controls and no passcode, so send students the staff page, never an admin link.

  • Sections. The list on the left jumps to Space, Projects, Equipment, the Experiential Learning Lab, Fabman, Compliance and Fleet. This guide follows the same order.
  • Search. Press / and type. The arrow keys move through the results and Enter opens one.
  • Panels. The large tiles (Open issues dashboard, Review proposals, Inventory) open over the page. Full page opens the same view in its own tab, and Esc closes the panel.
  • Command Center. The gold button at the top. A number on it means something needs attention (see Command Center).
  • Developer page. The scripts, the code and the developer guide have their own page, linked at the bottom of the section list. Daily work never needs it.

02Space

Space Status is how staff report problems in team spaces and how teams are held to fixing them. It runs on the report form, the Space Status Tracking sheet and a script that sends the emails.

Report→ Email to the team→ Reminders→ Team sends a photo→ You approve

Deadlines and reminders

The answer to Respond within on the form sets the deadline. Reminders go to the same people as the first email.

Days are counted from the day of the report. Reminders pause while finished work waits for your review and stop once you approve it. Teams never see the color names.

Who gets the email

The team's row on the Team Contacts tab decides it. The email goes to the team's general email and its liaison, with the safety lead and anyone in Extra CC copied. Noah is copied on every email, and gets the email for any team with no address on file. Changes to Team Contacts apply from the next email; nothing needs restarting.

The open issues dashboard

Open issues dashboard lists every job across all teams. The chips at the top switch between open, pending and completed jobs, and search, the team picker and Overdue only narrow the list. The Projects tab at the top of the panel holds the projects (see Projects).

  • Mark complete (open jobs). Closes the job right away, with no photo and no review. Use it when you know the work is done.
  • Approve (pending jobs). The team has sent photos of the finished work, and maybe a note. Approving closes the job.
  • Send back (pending jobs). Reopens the job, with an optional reason. The team gets an email with your reason and the photos they sent, and reminders pick up again.
  • Edit. Change the team, issue type or details. Moving a job to another team emails that team a fresh notice and restarts its reminders. The required action stays as reported.
  • Delete. Removes the job from the sheet for good, after a confirm. There is no undo.

To reopen a job that was already approved, clear its Addressed at cell on the Form Responses tab.

What teams see

  • The button in the email opens a page where the team adds photos of the finished work and an optional note. The job then shows as pending for you.
  • Informational reports only need Mark done; a photo is optional. The same goes for older reports whose required action was a facilities work order, an unsafe practice correction, no action, or Other.
  • Operations Team email comes to an admin, so those jobs close as soon as they are marked complete, with no photo or review.
  • Share a team's issue link gives you a clean link to one team's live list of open issues, to send to that team.

The form and the sheet

  • Changing who gets email: edit the team's row on Team Contacts.
  • Adding a team: add the name to the form's Responsible Team question and add a row on Team Contacts with exactly the same name. Then ask a developer to run fillTeamTokens once; it gives the team its private page link.
  • Editing the form: do not rename the questions or the Respond within choices. The script reads both by their wording.
  • Columns the script fills in: Notified at, Issue token, Addressed at, Last reminder, Completion photo, Completion note, Completed at and Sent back reason. Leave them alone, apart from clearing Addressed at to reopen a job.

The Space Status manual explains when and how to report, for the people filing. The staff how-to shows what staff see when they finish a job.

03Projects

Projects are bigger jobs than a space issue, often for several people. They live on the Projects tab of the Space Status sheet, and staff find them under Your work on the staff page.

Running a project

  1. Create it in Assign & track projects, or the Projects tab of the open issues panel: a title, a description, any assignees, and if you like a before photo, a file or a link.
  2. Someone joins. The first person to join moves it from Assigned to In Progress.
  3. They finish it with an after photo and the hours it took. It moves to Pending.
  4. You review it. Approve marks it Completed. Send back returns it to In Progress with your reason; the photo and hours stay on it.

You can edit a project's title, description and assignees in place, or delete it (the row goes; its photos stay in Drive). Projects dashboard shows hours logged, the completion rate, top contributors and a before and after gallery.

Proposals

Staff post improvement ideas from Propose an improvement on the staff page, and the team rates them. Review proposals opens the same board with your controls on it.

  • Ratings. Each person rates Impact and Effort from 1 to 5. One rating per person per proposal (rating again replaces it), and nobody can rate their own.
  • Whose ratings count. A person's ratings count once they have rated 3 proposals. While fewer than 3 proposals are open, every rating counts.
  • Becoming a project. With 3 counted ratings and an average Impact of 3.5 or more, a proposal becomes an Open project by itself. Effort is there for planning and never holds an idea back.
  • Your controls. Edit fixes the title, description, area or name. Approve now makes it a project without waiting. Decline closes it but keeps the record. Delete removes it and its ratings, for junk.

A project that came from a proposal is announced on a gold strip at the top of the staff task pages for two weeks.

04Equipment

The inventory is an app in the hub with a Google Sheet behind it. Tools stay in the shops and nobody checks them out, so it records what there is, where each thing lives, what shape it is in, its maintenance, and how much of each supply is left. Staff open the same app from Tool inventory on the staff page, without the editing controls.

  • Inventory. Add and edit items: a photo (on a phone you can take one on the spot), the team that owns it or Shared, the location and the condition. On an item's page, Log maintenance records what was wrong or checked, what was done, by whom, the cost and when the next check is due. Supplies have Update the count.
  • Status. Condition is Good, Fair or Needs Repair. Setting Out of Service to Maintenance takes an item out of service; Retired items only show on the admin view. Supplies read In stock, Low stock or Out of stock against their minimum. The Maintenance due and Retired filters are admin only.
  • Print item labels. A QR label for every item, and scanning one opens that item. Item IDs (PT-001, EQ-004 and so on) never change, so printed labels stay good.
  • Inventory spreadsheet. The Dashboard tab has the same numbers as the app. Inventory Master has one row per item, the Maintenance Log has every repair and check, and Lists holds the dropdown choices, teams and locations included. Status, Next Maintenance and Item Link fill themselves in; don't type in them.
  • Test data. Load test data adds 26 sample items tagged [test data]. Reset and Remove only touch tagged items, so real ones are safe.

05Experiential Learning Lab

ELL staff fill in the ELL Shift Summary form at the end of each shift: how busy it was, how each room was used, supplies, and any safety or maintenance problems. View shift responses opens the answers. The raw rows are on the Form Responses 1 tab, with a summary view beside them. The Command Center flags safety problems and supply shortages from the latest shift.

06Fabman

Open Fabman goes to the Fabman admin site for members, activity, bookings and machine access. Bulk changes happen in the Fabman members sheet, through buttons that call Fabman for you.

  • Dashboard tab. Every Fabman member with their team, graduation year, apron and trainings, pulled from Fabman.
  • Sign-up Form tab. New sign-ups arrive here and are added to Fabman.
  • Actions tab. Put email addresses in column A, then press a button to make one change for all of them: grant Machines or Mills, set an apron color (Red also grants Machines and Mills), add or remove Supervisor, lock or activate accounts, delete them, or send Fabman invitations.
  • Graduates. Flag graduates for review lists everyone whose graduation year is this year or earlier on a Graduates tab, each ticked and set to Lock. Untick anyone to keep, switch someone to Delete if needed, then run Process graduates. Delete removes the Fabman account for good. Each row shows what happened in its Result column.

The Fabman-Canvas training integration doc maps the Canvas trainings to Fabman courses and apron tiers. The Fabman operations report covers procedures, courses and supervisor scheduling.

07Compliance

  • Building access & training records (the Master Sheet). Usage agreements and waivers signed through the Safety & Training Hub Canvas course, one tab per location (ELL, HVL, Rhodes penthouse and others), plus Workday EHS training completions.
  • Project Teams workspaces. The register of every team space: building, room, access form, waivers, usage agreement and safety plan, with the yearly renewal survey answers.
  • Summer contact list 2026. Who to reach on each team over the summer. View only.

08Fleet

Student drivers & reservations opens the sheet for the program's Tacoma (MBN-4258). The Approval tab tracks each driver's application and training, the Log tab holds the reservations, and the Process tab lists the approval steps.

Removing graduated drivers

  1. In that sheet, choose Driver cleanup > 1. List graduated drivers. Everyone graduating this year or earlier goes on a Drivers to remove tab.
  2. Check that tab and delete the row of anyone you want to keep.
  3. Choose 2. Delete listed drivers (rows) to take them off the drivers list, or 2. Remove listed drivers (access) to remove their edit access to the sheet.

The Command Center warns when the Tacoma is double-booked, when this week's reservations are missing details, and when driver applications are ready to file or stuck.

09Command Center

The Command Center shows what needs attention across every area on one screen: open, overdue and same-day space issues, ELL flags and supplies, Fabman members waiting for training and graduates who still have access, building access and Workday records, Tacoma bookings and driver applications, and mill room orders and locks not returned. The number on the gold button counts what needs attention. It reads the sheets directly and refreshes every few minutes.
